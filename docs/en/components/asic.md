# ASIC component

An ASIC component binds an ASIC project to an application setup. The ASIC
project defines the chip and owns its circuit and geometric products; the
component records the definition selected by the application.

The referenced ASIC definition connects its circuit model, physical geometry,
pads, bumps, and flip-chip assembly details. The application entry records its
placement and its electrical connection to the sensor or PCB used by the
scenario.

The ASIC project design is developed in the [ASIC draft](../core/draft/asic.md).
